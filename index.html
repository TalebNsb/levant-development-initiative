About Us

A bridge between people, communities and opportunities.

LEVANT DEVELOPMENT INITIATIVE is a non-profit and independent organization based in Malmö, Sweden.

Our work focuses on strengthening individuals, supporting local initiatives, encouraging entrepreneurship and creating meaningful cultural and social connections between Sweden and Syria.

We believe sustainable development begins with people who have the knowledge, confidence and opportunity to take initiative.

Sweden ↔ Syria

We connect knowledge, people, communities and opportunities across borders while respecting local contexts and needs.

What We Do

Turning knowledge and participation into action.

Our activities are designed to strengthen people, communities and local initiatives in Sweden and Syria.

01

Individual Development

Training, knowledge exchange and support that strengthen skills, leadership and opportunities for active participation.

02

Local Development

Supporting local initiatives and sustainable small-scale development projects that respond to community needs.

03

Entrepreneurship

Platforms, networks, advisory services and knowledge support for entrepreneurs and new development initiatives.

04

Cultural Exchange

Activities that strengthen friendship, dialogue, mutual understanding and social cohesion.

05

Community Support

Information, guidance and advisory support that promote participation and positive integration in Swedish society.

06

Partnerships

Cooperation with organizations, public authorities and other relevant actors to advance development and humanitarian goals.
