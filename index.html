About Us

A bridge between Sweden and Syria

We are building an independent platform for development, participation and cultural exchange.

LEVANT DEVELOPMENT INITIATIVE is a non-profit, independent and politically non-partisan association based in Malmö, Sweden.

Our purpose is to serve as a development and cultural bridge between Sweden and Syria, while creating opportunities for individuals and local communities to learn, participate and develop.

Our activities may take place in Sweden and internationally, including Syria, in accordance with Swedish legislation and applicable regulations in the countries where we operate.

Our approach

We focus on practical development, knowledge exchange, local participation and cooperation. We believe that sustainable change is strengthened when people have the knowledge, skills and opportunities to take part in shaping their communities.

What We Do

From individual empowerment to community development

Our areas of work reflect the purpose and principles of the association.

01

Individual Development & Empowerment

Supporting people to strengthen knowledge, skills, leadership and active participation through training, knowledge exchange, advisory support and development opportunities.

02

Small-Scale Development & Entrepreneurship

Initiating and supporting sustainable local development projects, entrepreneurship, networks and initiatives that can contribute to stronger communities.

03

Knowledge & Capacity Building

Creating opportunities for learning, knowledge exchange and capacity development for individuals, local initiatives and emerging development efforts.

04

Cultural & Social Relations

Strengthening cultural and social ties between Swedish and Syrian society through dialogue, mutual exchange, joint activities and social interaction.
